Choose a name, up to 16 characters.

hero.set_color("purple")

Use blue, red, green, purple, orange, pink, yellow, or white. A hex color like #22aaff works too.

Choose cap, crown, helmet, or none. Run these commands once. Your choices stay saved in this browser, even if you remove the commands. Run them again to change your look.

Hero commands

hero.move("right")

Move one tile. Use up, down, left, or right.

hero.attack("right", 3)

Hit an enemy next to you. Strength can be 1, 2, or 3.

hero.collect()

Pick up coins, keys, and potions on your tile.

hero.enemy_at("right")

Check for an enemy next to you.

hero.unlock("down")

Open the door next to you. Uses one key of the same color.

hero.activate()

Stand on a switch to open gates of the same color. Keys do not open gates.

hero.heal()

Use a potion to restore 5 health, up to 10.

hero.door_at("right")

Check for a locked door.

hero.can_move("right")

Check if the next tile is clear.

hero.on_coin / hero.on_key
hero.on_potion

Check for something to collect.

hero.health / hero.potions

Check your health or potion count.
